## The Method

We do not forecast events. We map **attractor basins**—stable states toward which complex systems tend to evolve given initial conditions and structural constraints.

The variables:
- **I**: Intelligence abundance (compute + algorithmic efficiency)
- **E**: Energy cost per joule (fusion, fission, solar+storage)
- **T**: Trust infrastructure (verifiable identity, provenance, reputation)
- **G**: Governance adaptation speed (institutional latency vs. tech velocity)
- **D**: Demographic structure (dependency ratios, youth bulges, aging)
- **C**: Coordination capacity (ability to solve collective action problems)

Each scenario is a region in this phase space. Probabilities are conditional on current trajectories.

## Scenario Space

### Basin A: Adaptive Integration (p ≈ 0.35)

**Structure**: Institutions bend but don't break. AI productivity gains are captured, taxed, redistributed. New social contracts emerge.

**Dynamics**:
- **2026–2028**: AI agents deploy in enterprise. Productivity measurable. Tax base shifts from labor to compute/energy.
- **2028–2030**: First AI taxation regimes (EU, Singapore, UAE). Universal Basic Services (compute credits, energy dividends, housing) replace UBI debates.
- **2030–2033**: Education restructures around *direction* not *retention*. Apprenticeship models return. Credential inflation reverses.
- **2033–2036**: New institutional forms: algorithmic auditing bureaus, synthetic media courts, compute commons.

**Psychology**: Managed anxiety → pragmatic adaptation. Status reallocates from "knowing" to "judging." Meaning shifts from occupation to contribution.

**Failure mode**: Capture. Elites monopolise compute/energy. Taxation fails. Basin A collapses into Basin C.

---

### Basin B: Fragmented Acceleration (p ≈ 0.25)

**Structure**: No global coordination. Jurisdictional arbitrage dominates. Speed wins. Regulation trails by 5–7 years.

**Dynamics**:
- **2026–2028**: Open-weight models reach GPT-5/6 capability. Sovereign AI stacks emerge (US, China, EU, India, Gulf, Singapore).
- **2028–2030**: Regulatory fragmentation. Some jurisdictions become "AI havens" (low tax, low oversight, high compute). Talent and capital flow there.
- **2030–2033**: Synthetic media saturates open internet. Trust infrastructure (T) becomes private: verified enclaves, reputation markets, blockchain-anchored provenance.
- **2033–2036**: Two internets: **VerifiedNet** (high trust, gated, expensive) and **WildNet** (anonymous, synthetic, free). Physical world mirrors: gated communities vs. ungoverned zones.

**Psychology**: Cynicism → tribalism. In-group trust high, out-group trust near zero. "Reality" becomes subscription-tier.

**Failure mode**: Cascade. A synthetic crisis (financial, biological, nuclear near-miss) forces emergency centralisation → Basin C or D.

---

### Basin C: Authoritarian Capture (p ≈ 0.20)

**Structure**: Crisis (economic, biological, conflict) legitimises emergency powers. AI becomes instrument of control. Compute centralised.

**Dynamics**:
- **2026–2028**: Major disruption: sovereign debt cascade, pandemic, or kinetic conflict. Emergency measures enacted.
- **2028–2030**: Compute nationalised or licensed. AI deployment requires state approval. Social credit / digital ID universalised "for safety."
- **2030–2033**: Productivity gains captured by state-capital fusion. Dissent algorithmically predicted, preemptively managed. Human labour directed by planning algorithms.
- **2033–2036**: Stable equilibrium. High material provision, zero political agency. Innovation permitted only in state-approved vectors. Demographic engineering via incentives/coercion.

**Psychology**: Fear → relief → resignation. Meaning externalised to collective projects. Private life minimised.

**Failure mode**: Brittleness. No error correction. Exogenous shock (climate, resource, external conflict) causes catastrophic collapse → Basin D.

---

### Basin D: Systems Cascade (p ≈ 0.15)

**Structure**: Multiple simultaneous failures exceed institutional absorption capacity. Complexity collapse.

**Dynamics**:
- **2026–2028**: Compound stressors: energy shortage + food price spike + synthetic financial crisis + cyber-physical attack.
- **2028–2030**: Supply chains fragment. Grid reliability drops. State capacity evaporates in periphery. Private security / warlordism in vacuum.
- **2030–2033**: AI infrastructure degrades (no spare parts, no specialised labour, no stable power). Knowledge loss: critical tacit knowledge holders die/retire without replacement.
- **2033–2036**: Neo-feudalism. Local strongmen control water, energy, compute fragments. Long-distance trade collapses. Population decline.

**Psychology**: Denial → panic → survival mode → localised meaning. Trust radius shrinks to kin/clan.

**Failure mode**: None. This *is* the failure mode. Recovery possible only on century timescales.

---

### Basin E: Post-Scarcity Transition (p ≈ 0.05)

**Structure**: Fusion breakthrough + AI-driven materials science + robotics converge. Marginal cost of goods → near zero.

**Dynamics**:
- **2026–2028**: Net-energy fusion (ITER/SPARC/private). AI discovers room-temp superconductor, carbon-negative cement, biological manufacturing.
- **2028–2030**: Autonomous factories. Food, shelter, compute, transport approach marginal cost ~energy. Universal Basic Compute + Energy enacted globally.
- **2030–2033**: Labour decoupled from survival. Status economy explodes: reputation, creativity, care, exploration, craft become primary currencies.
- **2033–2036**: New human project: expansion (orbital, biological, cognitive). Governance restructures around resource allocation not scarcity management.

**Psychology**: Existential vacuum → renaissance. Crisis of meaning as survival pressure lifts. New religions/philosophies emerge.

**Failure mode**: Capture (→ Basin C) or meaning collapse (→ Basin D via wireheading/simulation retreat).

## Cross-Cutting Structural Forces

These operate *across* basins, modifying probabilities:

| Force | Effect on Basins |
|-------|------------------|
| **Demographic inversion** (aging Global North, youth Global South) | Favours B (migration pressure), C (authoritarian border control), D (state failure in youth-bulge regions) |
| **Climate nonlinearity** (tipping points: AMOC, permafrost, ice sheets) | Increases D probability. Forces coordination (A) or fragmentation (B/C) |
| **Nuclear latency** (more actors, AI-assisted weapons design) | Increases C (security state) and D (accident/escalation) |
| **Biological vulnerability** (synthetic pathogens, democratised bio) | Strongly favours C (biosecurity state) or A (if verification infrastructure built) |
| **Compute geography** (energy + cooling + talent + sovereignty) | Concentrates power. Who hosts the intelligence hosts the future. |

## The Psychohistorian's Read

**Most probable trajectory**: **Basin A with B characteristics** — adaptive integration in core zones, fragmented acceleration at margins. A patchwork, not a monolith.

**Key pivot points** (where small inputs shift basins):
1. **2027–2028**: First AI taxation law in major economy (EU AI Act implementation, US executive action)
2. **2029**: First synthetic media crisis affecting election/financial market
3. **2030–2031**: Fusion net-energy or definitive failure
4. **2032**: Demographic inflection — Global South youth peak, Global North dependency peak

**What shifts probability toward A**:
- Early compute taxation / redistribution agreements
- Open verification standards (provenance, identity) adopted voluntarily
- Education reform preceding labour displacement
- Energy abundance breakthrough

**What shifts probability toward C/D**:
- Crisis exploited for permanent emergency powers
- Compute concentrated in <5 entities globally
- Trust infrastructure not built before synthetic saturation
- Major war between AI-integrated powers

## Implications for the Individual

Psychohistory offers no personal salvation. But it identifies **robust strategies**—actions that improve position across *multiple* basins:

1. **Compute access** — Own or have guaranteed access to inference/training capacity
2. **Energy security** — Local generation, storage, or contractual priority
3. **Verified reputation** — Cryptographically anchored, portable across jurisdictions
4. **Multi-jurisdictional footprint** — Legal, financial, physical presence in ≥3 basins
5. **Tacit knowledge mastery** — Skills that survive compute loss (medicine, agriculture, repair, mediation, defence)
6. **High-trust networks** — Kin, clan, tribe, guild — people who coordinate without contracts
7. **Psychological anti-fragility** — Meaning structures independent of external validation

## The Unspoken Variable

There is a variable not in our equations: **Human agency at the margin.**

Psychohistory assumes mass behaviour follows statistical laws. But *critical individuals at critical junctures* can shift basin probabilities by 1–2%. Over a decade, that compounds.

The next Marcus Aurelius, Ibn Khaldun, or Lee Kuan Yew is currently 15–35 years old. They are reading this, or something like it. Their decisions in 2028, 2030, 2033 will retroactively determine which basin we inhabit.
